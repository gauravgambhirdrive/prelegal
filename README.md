# Prelegal

Prelegal is a legal document management platform designed to help teams organize, track, and manage legal documents efficiently. It streamlines the document lifecycle from creation to archival, providing a centralized system for all legal paperwork.

## Problem Statement

Legal teams often struggle with scattered documents across email, shared drives, and physical files. Prelegal solves this by providing a single source of truth for all legal documents, reducing the risk of working with outdated versions and improving collaboration across teams.

## Features

- **Document Organization** — Categorize and tag legal documents for easy retrieval
- **Version Tracking** — Maintain a full history of document revisions with diff comparison
- **Search & Filtering** — Quickly find documents by type, date, status, or keyword
- **Access Control** — Role-based permissions to ensure document confidentiality
- **Status Tracking** — Monitor document workflows from draft to approval
- **Audit Trail** — Complete log of who accessed or modified each document
- **Template Management** — Reusable templates for common legal document types (NDAs, contracts, agreements)
- **Deadline & Reminder Alerts** — Automated notifications for contract renewals and filing deadlines
- **Collaboration** — Multi-user editing and review workflows with commenting support
