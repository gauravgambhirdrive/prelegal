# Prelegal

Prelegal is a legal document management platform designed to help teams organize, track, and manage legal documents efficiently. It streamlines the document lifecycle from creation to archival, providing a centralized system for all legal paperwork.

## Features

- **Document Organization** — Categorize and tag legal documents for easy retrieval
- **Version Tracking** — Maintain a full history of document revisions
- **Search & Filtering** — Quickly find documents by type, date, status, or keyword
- **Access Control** — Role-based permissions to ensure document confidentiality
- **Status Tracking** — Monitor document workflows from draft to approval
